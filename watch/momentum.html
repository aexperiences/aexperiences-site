Momentum OS — what it actually is

Prepared for Josh and Katie Terra

Momentum OS is one system with more than thirty apps inside it. The Team App is one of them — it is already built and it is already in there. Scheduling, check‑in, the time clock, payroll, tuition and billing, the books: every one of them is its own app, and every one of them talks to the others. Nothing gets entered twice.

Add anything, remove anything, change anything. It stays one system, one login, one bill.
