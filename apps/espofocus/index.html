See whether the plan is working.

Each week, you and your child's teacher or behavior specialist rate the same 15 things. ESPOfocus charts the trend so the question “is this helping?” becomes a line you can see — and share with your provider.

Not a diagnosis. ESPOfocus is a private monitoring aid to bring to your provider — it doesn't diagnose or treat. Based on the widely used weekly ADHD monitoring report (SNAP‑IV/Vanderbilt lineage).
