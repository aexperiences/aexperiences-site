A word game with the people you actually know.

Take turns building words on the board. Nothing interrupts you: there are no ads, no coins, no power-ups, no treasure chests, no strangers, and no chat for anyone to work you in. You play the people you invite, and that is the whole product.

We ask for an email and a password so your games follow you between your phone and your laptop. That is the only reason. We do not send email, we do not sell or share anything, and there is no third-party tracking in this page.

Because we do not send email, you will get a recovery code on the next screen. That code is the only way back into your account if you forget your password. Keep it somewhere.

How the board works

Our own board and our own tiles, not a copy of anyone's. 60 premium squares, arranged symmetrically. The centre square pays no bonus, so going first is not an advantage. Using all seven tiles in one turn pays 40. Tiles are drawn from the bag at random with the same shuffle for both players, and neither of us can see the bag.

What we do not do

No ads, anywhere, ever — not between turns, not to see your own move, not for a reward. No coins, no power-ups, no hint tools sold back to you. No bots dressed up as people: every opponent here is a person you invited. No stranger matchmaking and no chat, which is how the scams in these games find people. No analytics, no ad SDKs, no third-party scripts on this page. We do not sell or share anything, because we do not collect anything to sell.

Your email is a login and nothing else. We never send to it.

Something wrong?

If a word was rejected that you believe is real, or anything here behaves badly, that is a bug and we fix it. This game exists because the big ones stopped being pleasant to play.
