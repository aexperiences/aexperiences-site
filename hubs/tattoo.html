Questions, answered straight

What you’re probably wondering.

Do I own the software?
No, and that is deliberate. You licence it, the way you licence every other tool you run on. What you own outright is your content, your data and your brand — exportable any time, including the day you leave. We build, host, maintain and keep improving the software for a flat monthly, so you are never left maintaining it yourself.
Are there per-seat or per-transaction fees?
No per-transaction fee — you connect your own payment processor and keep 100% of your revenue. Seats are unlimited on every tier — it never costs more when you hire somebody.
Is it really built for tattoo studio?
Yes. The Sleeves OS is configured to how tattoo studio actually works — not a generic template. We tailor it to your business before it goes live.
How long does it take to launch?
Most builds go live within a few weeks. We start with the smallest version that works on your real data, prove it, then expand — so you see something real early.
Can I move off my current software?
Yes. Data migration starts at $1,500 and we bring your existing records over, so you are not starting from a blank slate.
Do you offer non-profit pricing?
Yes — 30% off monthly for mission-driven organizations, plus founder (20% off for life + 50% off white-label) and annual-prepay (2 months free) programs.
