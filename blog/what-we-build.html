The Shop

A Small Family Business That Builds Big Software

Operating systems, specialized web apps, websites, stores, point of sale, market analysis, targeted marketing. A father, a nurse, and four kids who keep us honest.
Anthony EspositoJuly 2, 20262 min read

Accelerated Experiences LLC is a family business in Post Falls, Idaho. A father, a nurse, and four kids who keep us honest. No sales floor, no account managers, no phone tree — the person who built your system is the person who answers about it.

Here's the honest spread of what we do, because it's wider than one shelf:

  • Complete business operating systems — fourteen industries so far, from dental practices to theaters to home-care agencies. The whole operation in one place: schedule, money, pipeline, paperwork.
  • Specialized web apps — purpose-built tools that do one job extremely well, for a business or for one person.
  • Websites and online stores — built, hosted, and maintained, with the selling wired in from day one.
  • Point of sale and taking payment — box office seats, in-store checkout, deposits and invoices, built into the systems instead of bolted on.
  • Market analysis and targeted marketing — the twenty-one-year craft this company was built on: real census and consumer data, segmentation, and campaigns pointed at the people who actually buy.
Solutions for everyone — a solo chair, a family shop, a multi-office firm. Sized to the operation, never per-seat.

The reason a small shop can carry a list like that is focus in the build: one store where everything lives, one price record, one standard of quality, and no demos anywhere — everything on the shelf is the real, working thing. You don't book a call to find out what we do. You walk in and click it.

If your operation needs something that isn't on a shelf yet, that's the other half of the family business: we build to order, and what we build for you gets maintained like everything else here — better than yesterday, never finished.
