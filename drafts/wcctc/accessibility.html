Accessibility statement

Conformance target: WCAG 2.1 Level AA, the standard named in the Department of Justice's 2024 rule for state and local government websites (28 CFR 35.200). No overlay widget. The accessibility is in the markup, and it is tested by hand as well as by machine.

The commitments

Accessibility commitments
CommitmentHow
One heading structure per pageA single H1 that names the page; the logo is a link with a text alternative, not a heading
Skip link and visible focusFirst element on every page; a 3-pixel amber outline on every control
Alternative textWritten for every image at migration; decorative images marked as such
Color and contrastWCCTC blue on white 8.3:1; green used for bars and accents, never for text on white
Meeting materialsAgendas and staff reports in HTML as well as PDF; packets tagged before posting
DocumentsA posting workflow that checks title, language, tags and reading order; archive material labeled with a request link
FormsEvery field labeled; errors described in words next to the field
LanguageSpanish and Chinese as real pages for the most-used content; the switcher on every page
TestingAutomated (axe-core) plus keyboard-only and screen-reader passes on key templates before launch, and an annual re-check

Findings on the current site

Read off westcontracostatc.gov on September 4, 2026:

  • Two hidden <h1> elements on every page read West Contra Costa Transportation Advisory Committee — the wrong organization name, announced twice before the page's own title (WCAG 1.3.1, 2.4.6).
  • No skip link (2.4.1).
  • Homepage: six of nine images have no alternative text (1.1.1) — a known issue the RFP names.
  • Meeting pages carry a single PDF packet and no HTML agenda; the most-used content is the least accessible.
  • Keyboard focus relies on the browser default only (2.4.7).

None of these is an opinion; each is fixed in this draft and can be checked in the markup.

Report a barrier

If any part of this site is hard to use, email JNemeth@WestContraCostaTC.gov or call (510) 215-7200. Barriers are logged and answered within five business days.
