A finding on the current site

Every page of westcontracostatc.gov carries two hidden level-one headings that read West Contra Costa Transportation Advisory Committee — the wrong name, announced twice by a screen reader before anything else. There is no skip link, and on the homepage six of nine images have no alternative text. Read off the live markup September 4, 2026.
