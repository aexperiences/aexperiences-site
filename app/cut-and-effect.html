What it is

ESPOfunkmaster and ESPO Cutlabs as one app, with Roz doing the cutting. Your son and daughter shoot a movie — ten takes, three phones. Drop every take in. Roz puts them in the order they were shot, trims the dead ends, cross-fades between them, lays an AE beat under it, turns the beat down under the talking, lands a boom on the hits, adds a title card and an end card, and tells you what she did and how sure she is. Drop in photos instead and she makes a slideshow.

Then talk to her — hold the red button and say it, or type it. “Make it faster.” “Add a boom here.” “Black and white.” “Make her eyes green.” “Different music.” “Call it Beach Day.” She changes it and reads it back. Eight templates — kids’ movie, birthday, vacation, memories, music video, vlog, slideshow — or free style with the raw takes. FunkMaster is inside for making your own beat and sending it straight to the movie.

Press Save and it writes an MP4 to the camera roll. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is rendered somewhere else, and after the first visit it works with no Internet at all — the app, the beats and the face model are kept on the phone.

Cut & Effect runs in your browser. There is nothing to download and no account — the link above opens the real software, not a preview or a video of it.
