What you are buying

A monthly subscription to a business operating system from the AE App Shop. It runs in your browser — there is nothing to install and nothing to download. You get the whole system named on the Buy button, for your whole business, at one flat monthly price. It never costs more when you hire somebody, because we do not charge per person. And whatever your business earns inside it is yours: we never take a cut.

What it costs

The price on the Buy button is the price. Prices are in US dollars and do not include sales tax. If tax applies where you are, you see it on the checkout page before you pay — never as a surprise afterward.

How you pay

By card, through Stripe, right on the store. You are charged the day you subscribe and on that same day each month after that, until you cancel. Your receipt lands in your email every time. We never see your card number and we never store it — Stripe handles that part, the same way it does for millions of other businesses.

Getting your system

After checkout, we set up your copy under your business name and email you your sign-in. You will hear from us directly — a person, not a form letter. If you have not heard from us within one business day, email us and we will sort it out.

Changing your mind

Cancel any time. Send one email to anthonye@aexperiences.studio from the address on your receipt and say “cancel.” We cancel it the same business day and send you a confirmation. Your system stays open until the end of the month you already paid for, and you are not charged again. That is the whole process.

Refunds

Your first month. If the system is not right for you, tell us within fourteen days of your first charge and we refund it in full. No questions, no forms.

After that. Once a month has started, that month is not refunded — cancel and the next charge simply never happens. You keep the system until the paid month runs out.

Our mistake. If we charged you the wrong amount, charged you twice, or your system was down because of something we did, we refund that month. Just tell us.

Where it goes. Refunds go back to the card you paid with. We send it the day we agree it; your bank usually takes five to ten business days to show it.

Your data

It is yours. Your clients, your jobs, your numbers — all of it. If you ever leave, ask and we send you an export of your records before we close your copy. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for anything except running your system.

Apps from the app stores

This page covers the business operating systems you buy here on aexperiences.com. Apps you get through the Apple App Store or Google Play are bought under those stores’ own terms and refunded through them.

Something wrong?

Email us first: anthonye@aexperiences.studio. A person answers. We would rather fix it than argue about it — and if we owe you money, we would rather send it than make you fight your card company for it.
